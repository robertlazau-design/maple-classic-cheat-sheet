# MapleStory Classic World: verified facts, contradictions and open questions

**What this file is**
- Compiled Oct 20, 2026 (day before Grand Launch) from six research passes:
  - official notes via recaps
  - community findings from Founder's Access
  - a fact-check of this site
  - citizenship and housing
  - events and deadlines
  - Lv60+ training and the Free Market
- Re-check anything here after each patch.

**Tags**
- [official]: Nexon notice or slide, including via a recap.
- [multi]: two or more independent sources agree.
- [single]: one source or datamined client data.
- [anecdote]: player reports.
- [model]: NiaMeowDB formula or estimate.

**Rule of thumb:** client data shows what exists, not what the server does. Event rewards, the house price, trade tax and drop rates are server-side.

## 2. World and core rules [official unless noted]

**World and accounts**
- One world, **Windia**, with 20 channels. 3 character slots per account. Deleting one starts a waiting period (about 5 min per meowdb).
- Level cap **100**. 1st job at Lv10 for every class, Magician included. 2nd job at Lv30 from the same instructor.
- Instructors: Dances with Balrog, Grendel, Athena Pierce, Dark Lord.
- Equipment you can get stops at **Lv50** for now.

**What's in the game**
- Launch content is Maple Island and Victoria Island only. No Ossyria, Aqua Road, Ludibrium or Leafre.
- Five bosses: Mano, Mushmom, Shade, Zombie Mushmom, Jr. Balrog. King Slime is KPQ-only, and Rotten Mushmom arrives Nov 18. Stumpy, Faust, Dyle, King Clang and Crimson Balrog aren't reachable.
- Crafting from Lv10. Citizenship from Lv12, Henesys or Kerning.

**Chat and megaphones**
- Chat accepts English, Spanish and Korean characters. Megaphones are English-only.
- Megaphone 350 NX (channel). Super Megaphone 1,500 NX (world). Item Megaphone 1,500 NX.

**Getting around**
- The boat from Maple Island leaves at Lv7, costs 300 mesos and is one-way (Shanks).
- Main MapleStory and Classic can't run on one PC at the same time.

**Founder's Packages**
- Cosmetic only. Claim from the mailbox by Nov 30 23:59 UTC, once per account per platform.
- You must leave Maple Island before the mail works. Open each claimed box within 30 days.
- Don't refund through Steam: it can lock the whole Nexon account [anecdote].

## 3. Character, combat and classes

**Stats and points**
- **AP:** every stat starts at 4 and you place 9 free points (12/5/4/4 is the usual pick, not a rule). +5 AP per level. No dice. [multi]
- **SP:** 3 per level from Lv10, +1 per job advancement. 2nd-job SP can't go into 1st-job skills. [multi]
- **HP/MP per level:** Warrior 28/12, Bowman/Thief 22/17, Magician 16/22. INT gives no MP and there's no HP washing. [multi]
- **Job advancement adds +500 HP/MP total:** Warrior +350/+150, Mage +150/+350, Bowman/Thief +250/+250. [multi]
- **AP or SP Reset:** 7,000 NX each.

**Combat rules**
- Accuracy scales with level, and magic can miss.
- Everyone can crit: base 5% rate and 20% damage. Gloves give +1–4% crit rate.
- Damage cap is 99,999.
- Elemental modifiers are halved: weak ×1.25, resist ×0.75. [multi]
- **AoE:** Slash Blast, Rush, Iron Arrow, Arrow Bomb, Poison Breath and Thunder Bolt hit **6**. Only 3rd-job AoEs were cut to 4. Ignore guides that say "most hit 4" (closed-test info). [multi]
- **Final Attack:** 25% proc on all branches and costs MP (it's a timed buff). Polearm FA rose 260% → 350%; Bow FA fell to 120%. [multi]

**Class changes**
- **Holy Arrow:** 1 arrow, 1 target, attack 100–160 (was 3 arrows on up to 3 targets).
- **Fire Arrow:** single target with a 3 s burn.
- **Bless:** −15% damage taken for the party (no longer ACC/avoid).
- **Heal:** recovery 300% → 100%. Range cut Oct 8.
- **Rush:** doesn't push bosses (Oct 15).
- **Arrow Bomb:** 2 s stun.
- **Steal:** must hit (Oct 15). Buff lasts 60 s.
- **Burn/bleed:** gave double EXP and drops until Oct 15, so ignore F/P and Fighter pace reports from before then.
- **Holy Symbol:** no EXP bonus. It's a self-revive buff (3rd job).
- **Haste:** +30 speed, +10 jump.

**Death**
- No penalty in towns [official].
- NiaMeowDB model: lose (5 + 20/base LUK)% of the EXP to next level. That's 10% at 4 LUK and 6% at 20. You can't de-level. A Safety Charm prevents it (800 NX, expires after 90 days). [model]

**Party EXP**
- Only members active on the map in the last **10 s** qualify (attacked, got hit or used a skill). [multi]
- Bonus model: 2 players +10–15%, 3 players +20–30%, 4 players +30–45%, 5 players +30–50%, 6 players +30–55%. Not official. [model]
- Metaroad's closed-test table was +10/+20/+30, capped at 4. [single, stale]

## 4. Scrolls [multi]

- **Weapon attack:** Lesser 100% +1, Intermediate 60% +2, **Greater 10% +4, Chaos 10% +5**. Greater and Chaos were +3/+4 in the closed test.
  - Average gain per slot: 1.0, 1.2, 0.4, 0.5. **Intermediate is the best value.**
- **Magic attack** (staff, wand): +2 / +4 / +8 / +10.
- **Stat scrolls:** +1 / +2 / +3 / +4.
- **HP/MP scrolls:** +10 / 15 / 20 / 25.
- Every attempt uses a slot. Only Chaos can destroy an item, and half of failed Chaos attempts do. 52 families, 208 scrolls.
- El Moth sells Lesser weapon scrolls for 30,000.
- Lesser cape, earring and glove scroll recipes cost a 20k fee.
- Arcforge Lesser recipes: hat Lv2, top Lv3, bottom Lv4, shield/overall DEF Lv5, shoes Lv6, cape DEF Lv7. Cape stat (Lv10) and glove (Lv9) are **locked**, because crafting caps at Lv8 at launch.

## 5. Leveling and EXP

**EXP table**
- Same as pre-Big Bang through Lv89, then ×2.1 per level from 90.
- 60→70 = 15.56M (about the same as 1→60, 15.98M). 70→80 = 26.5M. 80→90 = 45.2M. 90→100 = 18.09B (not reachable). Lv99→100 alone is 9.48B. [single, meowdb table]
- Quest EXP: 246 quests changed EXP at release, mostly up. 218 changed mesos.
- Monsters: 62 retuned at release (40 give less EXP, 39 harder to hit). King Slime EXP 248 → 2,400.

**Accuracy (NiaMeowDB formula, implemented in site.js `phit`)**
- L = max(0, mobLv − yourLv); A = ACC×100/(10L+255).
- f = 0.15 + 0.20/(1+e^((A−avoid)/12)); q = avoid/A.
- If q ≤ 1−f you always hit. If q ≥ 1+f you never hit. Otherwise p = (1+f−q)/(2f).
- Being under a monster's level costs accuracy. At or above it, extra levels don't help you hit.
- Live play [anecdote]: players trained 4–8 levels over their target (Lorang 42+, Cold Eye 43+, Copper Drake 47+, Drake 48+).

## 7. KPQ [multi]

- **Party:** Lv21+ with no upper cap (observed). Exactly 4 players, 30 minutes.
- **Channels:** 3–7 only.
- **Rules:** multiple parties per channel, and death doesn't end the run. It does end if the party drops to 2.

## 8. Citizenship and housing

**Joining**
- Lv12. Henesys (**Arthur**, Town Hall) or Kerning City (**Roxy**, Civic Center). One town at a time. [official]
- Grades never decay. Points only count for your active town, and your progress in the old town is kept.

**Grades (Public Release client)** [multi]
- Each grade needs a level and points. Points don't carry from grade to grade, so 128,000 in total.

|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Traveler | 12 | sign-up |
| 2 | Visitor | 17 | 1,500 |

| 9 | Guardian of the Village | 52 | 26,000 |
| 10 | Citizen of Honor | 57 | 32,000 |

- COT2 used 1,000 to 10,000. Some guides still quote those.
- **Discounts:** shops and taxi 5→25% (+2% per grade, 25% at grade 10). Storage 5→50% (fee 95→50 mesos per item).
  - Nexon's wording: "cheaper cabs, Storage Keeper fees and **some** general store items".
- **Daily points:** one board neighbor a day, 100 + 25×(grade−1).
  - VIP neighbors from grade 5 (Lv32) pay ~5,850 EXP: Chief Stan + Athena (Henesys), Chun Ji + Dark Lord (Kerning).
- **Weekly points:** donate **30** of one ETC item for 700–2,100 points + EXP + mesos + 20 Blue Potions.
  - Better items need higher grades. Old guides say 100 items; that's COT2.
- **Pace** [single, maplestory.quest]: about 8 days to grade 2, then 14/20/26/32/37/42/48/54 days per grade. About 281 days to grade 10.
- **Story arcs:** 5 quests each, at Lv17 (grade 2), 27 (G4), 37 (G6), 47 (G8) and 57 (G10).
  - 123 citizenship quests in total. "72 quests" was a wiki import count.
  - An arc needs that neighbor's First Greeting first.
  - The Lv57 arcs give the town earrings: Lv57, M.DEF 42, +2% crit damage, 5 slots, untradeable.
- **Caretaker's Ledger** is a regular Ellinia Lv28 chain, not citizenship: 24,540 EXP and 5,352 mesos, ends with the tradeable Faded Maple Leaf Earrings.

**Town perks**
- **Henesys:** Bronze Arrows at 2 mesos (**grade 3**), Supreme Sniper Potion (+7 ACC, 600), Sharpness Potion (+3% crit, G3), STR/DEX overall scrolls at G7. Flint's scrolls are all 100%.
- **Kerning:** Wolbi stars at 1,000 (G3), Supreme Dexterity Potion (+7 avoid), Destructive Potion (+3% crit damage, G3), LUK/INT overall scrolls at G7, Dr. Faymus discounts.
- **Both:** glove ATT/MATT 100% scrolls at G9, Elixir 3,000 at G10.
- Scroll prices conflict: meowdb says 40k, other sites 50k or 25k.

**Resident's Chair**
- 500,000 mesos at G2 (it was 10k in COT2). +20 HP / +5 MP every 10 s. Untradeable, one per town.

**Switching and gotchas**
- Switching costs **50,000–100,000 mesos** (sources disagree; the first switch may be free).
- Players lost citizenship to misclicks on the confirm window.

**Housing (Oct 21)**
- Requires grade 2 in Henesys or Kerning. [official]
- Homes: Yellow Mushroom House (Henesys) and Backalley Semi-Basement (Kerning), each behind a gold door.
- NPCs: estate agent Tommy / Jack, registrar Bianca / Scarlett, furniture Oak / Weston.
- **61 placeable pieces:** 52 furniture (13 interactive), 2 storage chests, 6 crafting stations and 1 straw training dummy.
- Return Scroll to My Home has a 60-min cooldown. You can visit friends; the client hints at lock/password options and pets roaming free.
- **Unknown:** the price, how you get furniture, chest capacity, and where to buy the return scroll.

## 9. Events

**Mark of the Beta**
- Lv25, from Oct 21, ends "in November". Starts with **Jay** in the middle of Henesys. Order:
  1. The Marks We Leave Behind (Jay gives the Adventurer's Journal)
  2. First Mark: the crack in the rocks at Pig Beach
  3. Second: monsters in Someone Else's House
  4. Third: an old tree high in Marr's Forest
  5. Fourth: Mong from Kong in Kerning sells the page for 10 mesos
  6. Fifth: 4 scraps; **location unconfirmed**
  7. Final: the hidden portal at the end of Ant Tunnel Park
- Reward: the Mark of the Beta hat (+1 all stats, 15 W.DEF, 7 slots, untradeable). EXP and mesos unknown.
- Closed-test players who finished both test events can claim the Classic Beginnings Label and Chat Ring; how is unknown.
- meowdb's "Mark of the Beta by Oct 23" banner is a promo for their own website, not the game.

**The Journey Begins / Inkwell's Gift**
- Lv10+ daily. Bring 30 Maple Leaves (any Lv10+ mob drops them) to the Maple Administrator in Henesys (far left) or Kerning.
- 14 gifts; it counts claims, not days. Resets at 00:00 UTC.
- The slide showed a Snail pet, potions, megaphones (Lv15 versions) and something shop-related. The per-day list is unknown.
- Likely ends ~Nov 18.

**Malady's Halloween**
- Lv10+, from Oct 28. Malady is on the left side of Kerning (GMS sprite: Witch Malady NPC 9201028).
  1. [Event] Halloween Candies: bring 100 from Lv10+ mobs. The text names Ribbon Pig, Dark Stump, Octopus and Green Mushroom.
  2. [Event] Trick or Treat!: deliver 3 pouches (the child in the dark tunnel, on the open field, up on the high ground).
  3. Return to Malady.
- Spare candy is exchanged at the Maple Administrator in Henesys.
- The candy item: untradeable, time-limited, stacks to 100, and may only drop with the quest active.
- Not repeatable as far as we know.
- In the client but unconfirmed as rewards: Red/Blue/Green Malady's Candy, Pumpkin Basket (sword), Halloween Broomstick Chair, and a Halloween fashion crate.
- **Unknown:** the reward, the exchange list, the drop rate, and the kids' maps.

**Earlier events**
- Founder's First Step (Oct 6–20): a check-in counter. Day 1 gave a 5,000 Leaf Points coupon, megaphones and more.
- GM events with Paul in Lith Harbor ran on Ch 10/13/16.

## 10. Economy, Free Market and Cash Shop

**Free Market**
- 11 rooms. Per the wiki, the portals are in Perion and Henesys Market; verify in game.
- No search or auction house; shops spill across channels. Return Scrolls, teleport rocks and Mystic Door are blocked inside.

**Store permits**

| Permit | Cost | Length | Items | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Regular | 1,500 NX | 90 d | 12 | |
| Fall | 3,000 NX | 90 d | 18 | until Nov 18 |
| Pink | 3,000 NX | 90 d | 18 | |
| Special (Monthly Essentials) | | | 24? | slot count unconfirmed |

- You must stay online. A shop opens in the FM or in a field, and closes if a monster hits you.
- Hired Merchants aren't sold (meowdb says "not in game yet"; they're in the client).

**Trading**
- Trade tax and trade limits are **unknown/unpublished**.
- Many Cash items are "tradeable once". Equipping a Cash item makes it untradeable.
- Coming: a 48 h trade/gift lock on Cash items after an account's first NX purchase. [official via Discord]

**Prices (NiaMeowDB, player-reported)**
- **NX to mesos:** 1.88 (Oct 7) → 8 (Oct 14) → 11.8 (Oct 18) → ~14–15 (Oct 19–20).
- **Listings vs sales:** listings run about 40% above sale prices, so price at the sold median.
- **Week one:** Cash outfits took 41% of mesos spent, while ores and drops were 39% of sales but only 3% of mesos.
- **Fast sellers (Oct 19):**
  - Squishy Shoes (~14k), Shoes Speed Greater (~15k), Pet Equip Speed Lesser (~22k)
  - Emerald Earrings (~130k), SP Reset (~80k), Cat's Eye (~29k)
  - Old Raggedy Cape (~10k), Selective 5-slot Coupon (~12.5k)
- **No sales:** Squishy Liquid, White Bandana, Evil Eye Tail.
- **Indexes:** the 60% scroll index peaked Oct 12 at 510 and is cooling; the commodity/ore index collapsed (~38).
- **Don't use U4N price lists**: no method, and the site sells mesos.
- **Third-party tools:** don't recommend market apps like "FM Sync"; their standing under Nexon's terms is unknown.

**Cash Shop**
- AP/SP Reset 7,000. Safety Charm 800.
- Convenience Essentials 9,900, once per account: AP + SP Reset, a Selective 5-slot Coupon, 10 Safety Charms, 10 Super Megaphones.
- Slot coupons 5,000. Pets 3,500 plus Water of Life 2,000 per 30 days. Gifting needs Lv12.

**Bots, RMT and bans**
- Hotspots: Henesys Hunting Ground, the Slime Tree, the Kerning subway.
- Report with right-click → Report. The daily cap is unconfirmed (~15 claimed).
- Bans: **1,379** permanent (first posted ~Oct 10–11; also dated Oct 17) and **3,121 by Oct 14**, real-money trading included.
- Bans cover the main GMS account too, with no exemption for Founder buyers.
