About this cheat sheet

A fan-made quick reference for MapleStory Classic World, built to stay open on your phone while you play.

Where the data comes from

We compile Classic-specific information from community databases and guides, chiefly MeowDB (becoming NiaDB), mapleclassic.wiki, Metaroad, maplestory.quest and launch-week player reports, and we link the source on each entry. All of it is rewritten in our own words.

How sure are we?

Confirmed several Classic sources agree. Likely one Classic source, or partly our estimate. Unconfirmed unverified, or content that isn't live yet.

Found a mistake?

Classic is new and numbers change with patches. Email robertlazau@gmail.com with what you saw in game and we'll fix it.
