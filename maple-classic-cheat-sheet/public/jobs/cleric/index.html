Cleric Build: MapleStory Classic

Support healer: Heal and Bless for parties, Holy Arrow for single-target damage, Invincible for solo survival.

MagicianWand or staff (+ shield)LikelyChecked Oct 19, 2026

AP buildSP buildJob advancementPros & cons

AP build

INT primary; keep base LUK at level minus 10 (4 INT / 1 LUK per level from Lv30).

  1. Lv1-30: as Magician.
  2. Lv31-70: 4 INT / 1 LUK per level (182/30 at Lv40, 222/40 at Lv50, 262/50 at Lv60, 302/60 at Lv70).
  3. Lv70-100 (our estimate): continue LUK = level - 10, or less with LUK gear.
  4. Gear: Green Goldwind Shoes (+5 LUK) at Lv50 cover LUK through Angel Wings.
  5. Pitfall: LUK above level-10 starves INT; skip Arc Staff (40 LUK too early on this plan).

SP build

1st job (levels 10–29)

  1. Same as Magician 1st-job plan.

2nd job (level 30+)

  1. Party build: Lv30 Heal 1; Lv31 Teleport 1, MP Eater 1, Holy Arrow 1; Lv32-41 Holy Arrow to 30; Lv42-44 Heal 5, Invincible 5, Bless 1; Lv45-49 Bless to 16; Lv50-58 Heal to 30; Lv58-64 MP Eater to 20; Lv64-70 Teleport to 20.
  2. Solo build: Heal 2 / Teleport 1 / MP Eater 1 at Lv30-31; Holy Arrow to 30 by Lv41; Heal 5 + Invincible to 20 by Lv49; MP Eater to 20 by Lv55; Teleport to 20 by Lv62; Heal to 30 + Bless 1 by Lv70.
  3. Lv71-100 (our estimate): finish Bless and Invincible (party) or Bless (solo).
  4. Classic changes: Holy Arrow now hits 1 target but M.ATK 160 (was 3 targets / 60 in test); Invincible reduces touch damage 20% at max; Bless -15% party damage taken at max.

Job advancement

1st job: level 10, Grendel the Really Old in Ellinia (Magic Library).

2nd job: level 30, Grendel the Really Old in Ellinia. Same Magician test (instructor at The Forest North of Ellinia, 30 Dark Marbles), then pick Cleric.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Highest physical and magical EHP in MeowDB Lv70 comparison
  • Heal and Bless are strong party support
  • Heal damages undead

Cons

  • Lowest single-target damage at Lv70
  • Holy Arrow is single-target only in Classic
  • Heal damage is much lower than pre-Big Bang; awkward targeting
