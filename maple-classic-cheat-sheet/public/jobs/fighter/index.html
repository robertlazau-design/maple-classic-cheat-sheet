Fighter Build: MapleStory Classic

Front-line melee bruiser: Rush mobs together, Power Strike singles, Slash Blast groups, keep Rage + Booster up.

WarriorSwords or axes (1H with shield, or 2H)LikelyChecked Oct 19, 2026

AP buildSP buildJob advancementPros & cons

AP build

STR primary; keep total DEX (base + gear) only at your next weapon's requirement. Lv1-10 all STR; Lv10-30 mostly 4 STR / 1 DEX; Lv31-36 3 STR / 2 DEX; Lv37-57 4 STR / 1 DEX; Lv58+ all STR once DEX reaches 60 base.

  1. Lv1-10: all AP into STR (about 57 STR / 5 DEX at Lv10). No DEX needed to advance.
  2. Lv10-30: ~4 STR / 1 DEX per level with a few 3/2 levels; target ~135 STR / 27 DEX at Lv30.
  3. Lv31-36: 3 STR / 2 DEX per level (~153 STR / 39 DEX at Lv36).
  4. Lv37-57: 4 STR / 1 DEX per level (~237 STR / 60 DEX at Lv57).
  5. Lv58-70: all STR (~302 STR / 60 DEX at Lv70). Lv70 2H weapons need 70 total DEX; cover the rest with gear.
  6. Lv70-100 (our estimate): keep DEX at weapon requirement, rest STR.
  7. Pitfall: adding DEX for damage or advancement is wasted; levels add ACC (~0.8 ACC per level vs 0.48 per DEX). Use Precise Strikes, gear and Sniper potions to fix accuracy.
  8. Pitfall: check gear DEX before adding base DEX; you may need less than 60.

SP build

1st job (levels 10–29)

  1. Beginner: Three Snails 3, Recovery 3, Nimble Feet 3 by Lv10
  2. Lv10-12: Power Strike 4, Slash Blast 3
  3. Lv13-14: Slash Blast 4, Precise Strikes 5
  4. Lv15-20: Power Strike to 20 (max), Slash Blast 6
  5. Lv21-25: Slash Blast to 20 (max), Precise Strikes 6
  6. Lv26-28: Precise Strikes to 15 (max)
  7. Lv29-30: Improved HP Recovery 3, Max HP Increase 3
  8. Skip: Iron Body. Don't rush Max HP Increase (no longer adds HP per level, so delaying costs nothing).
  9. 2nd-job SP cannot be spent on 1st-job skills: finish this by Lv30.

2nd job (level 30+)

  1. Lv30: Rush 1
  2. Lv31-38: Weapon Mastery (Sword or Axe) to 20; Booster 1 at Mastery 5; Final Attack 1-2
  3. Lv39-48: Rage to 30
  4. Lv49-57: Final Attack to 30
  5. Lv58-64: Booster to 20
  6. Lv64-70: Rush to 20 (1 SP spare at 70)
  7. Lv71-100 (our estimate): all Fighter skills are maxed by ~Lv70; extra SP has nowhere to go until 3rd job.
  8. Options: Booster 10 or Rush 10 earlier for upkeep/mobility. Don't push Rush early (Rush 1 costs 35 MP). Pick sword vs axe before spending Mastery SP. Turn Final Attack off when PS already one-shots.
  9. Classic changes: Final Attack proc 25% (was 50% in test), Rush moved into 2nd job (6 targets, 15 MP at max), Power Guard is 3rd job.

Job advancement

1st job: level 10, Dances with Balrog in Perion.

2nd job: level 30, Dances with Balrog (quest: The Warrior's Next Journey) in Perion. Take his letter to the Warrior Job Instructor at West Rocky Mountain IV, enter the hidden test map, kill mobs for 30 Dark Marbles, trade for The Proof of a Hero, return to Dances with Balrog and pick Fighter.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Best damage-per-MP of the warrior branches at Lv70 in MeowDB benchmarks
  • Rage is a party Attack Power buff (+40 ATK at max)
  • Widest weapon choice (1H/2H sword and axe)

Cons

  • Short reach and slow movement; takes lots of hits
  • Rage lowers your WDEF (-40 at max)
  • Mastery/Booster/Final Attack lock you to one weapon family
  • Power Guard moved to 3rd job in Classic
