Crossbowman Build: MapleStory Classic

Line-piercing ranged: Iron Arrow down long flat lanes, Final Attack/Double Shot on singles.

BowmanCrossbow (2H)LikelyChecked Oct 19, 2026

AP buildSP buildJob advancementPros & cons

AP build

DEX primary; STR = crossbow level minus 10. Lv1-15 all DEX; then 1 STR/4 DEX.

  1. Lv1-15: all DEX (5 STR / 82 DEX at Lv15).
  2. Lv16-30: +1 STR / +4 DEX (20 STR / 142 DEX).
  3. Lv31-70: +1 STR / +4 DEX (60 STR / 302 DEX). Gross Jaeger needs 60 STR.
  4. Lv70-100 (our estimate): STR to requirement, rest DEX.
  5. Pitfall: don't follow Hunter STR numbers; crossbows need 10 less.

SP build

1st job (levels 10–29)

  1. Same as Bowman 1st-job plan (Arrow Blow 20, Critical Shot 15, Eye of Amazon 15, Focus 10, PKB 1).

2nd job (level 30+)

  1. Lv30: Iron Arrow 1
  2. Lv31-39: Crossbow Mastery to 20, Booster 5, Soul Arrow 1, Final Attack 1
  3. Lv40-49: Iron Arrow to 30, Amazon's Judgement 1
  4. Lv50-54: Booster to 20
  5. Lv55-61: Soul Arrow to 20
  6. Lv62-70: Final Attack to 30
  7. Lv71-100 (our estimate): Amazon's Judgement to 20.
  8. Classic changes: Iron Arrow 6 targets, renamed (was Iron Arrow: Crossbow); Final Attack 25% proc.

Job advancement

1st job: level 10, Athena Pierce in Henesys.

2nd job: level 30, Athena Pierce in Henesys. Same Bowman test (Bowman Job Instructor, The Road to the Dungeon, 30 Dark Marbles), then pick Crossbowman.

Pros & cons

Pros

  • Iron Arrow pierces up to 6 mobs (raised from 4 in test)
  • Max Mastery adds +20% crit damage
  • Higher base W.ATK crossbows Lv50-70
  • Needs less STR than Hunter

Cons

  • Slower attack speed than bows (except Heckler Lv40)
  • Iron Arrow loses 20% damage per pierced mob
  • No movement skill; wants long flat maps
