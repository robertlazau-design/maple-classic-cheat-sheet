MapleStory Classic Job Advancement

1st job at level 10 with no stat requirement, 2nd job at level 30 after a short Dark Marble test. Here's who to see and where.

✓ Checked Oct 19, 2026Founder's Access data. Tags show how sure we are: Confirmed Likely Unconfirmed

Tip: ride the taxi to your job town while you're still a Beginner. Beginners get 90% off taxis.

2nd job (level 30)

Every class works the same way: your job NPC gives you a letter, the Job Instructor sends you into a test map, you collect 30 Dark Marbles, trade them for The Proof of a Hero and bring it back to choose your path.

JobWhoThe test
FighterDances with Balrog (quest: The Warrior's Next Journey), PerionTake his letter to the Warrior Job Instructor at West Rocky Mountain IV, enter the hidden test map, kill mobs for 30 Dark Marbles, trade for The Proof of a Hero, return to Dances with Balrog and pick Fighter.
PageDances with Balrog (quest: The Warrior's Next Journey), PerionSame Warrior test: letter to the Warrior Job Instructor at West Rocky Mountain IV, collect 30 Dark Marbles in the test map, bring The Proof of a Hero back and pick Page.
SpearmanDances with Balrog, PerionSame Warrior test (West Rocky Mountain IV instructor, 30 Dark Marbles, The Proof of a Hero), then pick Spearman.
Fire/Poison WizardGrendel the Really Old (quest: The Magician's Next Journey), ElliniaTake Grendel's letter to the Magician Job Instructor in The Forest North of Ellinia, enter the hidden test map, collect 30 Dark Marbles, trade for The Proof of a Hero, return to Grendel and pick F/P.
Ice/Lightning WizardGrendel the Really Old, ElliniaSame Magician test: instructor in The Forest North of Ellinia, 30 Dark Marbles, The Proof of a Hero, then pick I/L.
ClericGrendel the Really Old, ElliniaSame Magician test (instructor at The Forest North of Ellinia, 30 Dark Marbles), then pick Cleric.
HunterAthena Pierce, HenesysTake Athena's letter to the Bowman Job Instructor on The Road to the Dungeon (near Henesys), collect 30 Dark Marbles in the hidden test map, trade for The Proof of a Hero, return to Athena and pick Hunter.
CrossbowmanAthena Pierce, HenesysSame Bowman test (Bowman Job Instructor, The Road to the Dungeon, 30 Dark Marbles), then pick Crossbowman.
AssassinDark Lord (quest: The Thief's Next Journey), Kerning CityTake the letter to the Thief Job Instructor at the Construction Site north of Kerning City, collect 30 Dark Marbles in the test map, return The Proof of a Hero, pick Assassin.
BanditDark Lord, Kerning CitySame Thief test (Construction Site north of Kerning, 30 Dark Marbles, The Proof of a Hero), then pick Bandit.

3rd job

Not in Classic at launch. Nexon has said a 3rd job test server is planned for early 2027, alongside the Ossyria rework.
