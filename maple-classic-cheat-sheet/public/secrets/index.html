MapleStory Classic Hidden Maps & Secrets

Hidden streets, jump quests, boss rooms and one-time rewards on Maple Island and Victoria Island, with directions.

✓ Checked Oct 19, 2026Founder's Access data. Tags show how sure we are: Confirmed Likely Unconfirmed

Don't miss: Maple Island is one-way. Finish its quests (including Pio's boxes for the Green Relaxer chair) before you take the boat.

The Forest of Patience (jump quest, 5 steps)

Jump quest · Victoria Island - Ellinia (Hidden Street) · Level 25+ for Sabitrama's quest (anyone can pay to enter) Confirmed

How to get there: Talk to Shane in Ellinia; he charges an entry fee. Climb 5 obstacle stages to the herb at the top.

Why go: Pink Anthurium for Sabitrama's quest (Sleepywood, Lv25): 3,060 EXP, 804 mesos, +1 fame, random Overall Armor DEF scroll (Intermediate or Greater). Completing it unlocks free entry from Shane.

The Deep Forest of Patience (jump quest, 7 steps)

Jump quest · Victoria Island - Sleepywood (Hidden Street) · Level 50+ (Sabitrama part 2); 65+ (John's chain) Likely

How to get there: Use the Mysterious Statue NPC in Sleepywood town to warp in; 7 platforming stages. Sabitrama's Lv50 follow-up also routes via Shane in Ellinia and says the herb is deeper in the forest.

Why go: Sabitrama's Anti-Aging Medicine (Lv50): 12,900 EXP, 1,579 mesos, +2 fame, Star Rock, permanent free entry. John's Pink Flower Basket (Lv65, John in Lith Harbor, 10 Pink Violas): 25,900 EXP, 2,032 mesos, +1 fame, 30 Screws; 3-part chain (John's Present, John's Last Present).

Shumi's Lost Coin (Construction Site B1)

Jump quest · Victoria Island - Kerning City Subway · Level 35+ Confirmed

How to get there: Accept from Shumi in Kerning City (after Proof of Companionship). Enter the Kerning City Subway (Subway Ticketing Booth) and work down to Construction Site B1; the normal entrance is blocked, so you have to platform your way in.

Why go: 6,720 EXP, 1,111 mesos, +1 fame, 5 Nearest-Town return scrolls plus 3 each of Lith Harbor/Ellinia/Perion/Henesys/Kerning/Sleepywood return scrolls

Shumi's Lost Roll of Cash (Construction Site B2)

Jump quest · Victoria Island - Kerning City Subway · Level 35+ Confirmed

How to get there: Second step from Shumi; go deeper to Construction Site B2 (watch for moving machinery).

Why go: 6,720 EXP, 1,111 mesos, +2 fame, 30 Blue Potions

Shumi's Lost Sack of Cash (Construction Site B3)

Jump quest · Victoria Island - Kerning City Subway · Level 35+ Confirmed

How to get there: Final step from Shumi; reach Construction Site B3 at the bottom of the subway.

Why go: 6,720 EXP, 10,000 mesos, +3 fame, 30 Mana Elixirs (best payout of the three)

Someone Else's House (Mushmom spawn)

Boss map · Victoria Island - Henesys (Hidden Street) · Level 28-40 (Iron Hogs Lv28; Mushmom Lv40) Confirmed

How to get there: Henesys -> An Empty House -> Pig Park -> Pig Park II -> Someone Else's House (single portal from Pig Park II).

Why go: Mushmom (Lv40, 34,440 HP, 340 EXP); respawn about 1-1.5 h. Mushmom is cited as a source of high-tier glove attack scrolls. No Mystic Door or Teleport Rock here.

The Grave of Mushmom (Zombie Mushmom)

Boss map · Victoria Island - Sleepywood Dungeon · Level 24-55 (Zombie Mushrooms Lv24; boss Lv55) Confirmed

How to get there: Sleepywood -> Ant Tunnel I-IV -> Ant Tunnel Park -> The Grave of Mushmom.

Why go: Zombie Mushmom (Lv55, 66,640 HP, 668 EXP, Holy-weak, undead), respawn about 1-1.5 h. Reference drops (MSEA, unverified for Classic) include Intermediate attack scrolls. Zombie Mushrooms are good Cleric EXP.

The Cursed Sanctuary (Jr. Balrog)

Boss map · Victoria Island - Sleepywood Dungeon · Level 70+ realistically (boss Lv80) Confirmed

How to get there: Sleepywood dungeon -> Sanctuary Entrance I -> II -> III -> IV -> The Cursed Sanctuary. Entrance IV has Cold Eyes, Tauromacis and Taurospears.

Why go: Jr. Balrog (Lv80, 325,400 HP, 2,188 EXP, Holy-weak, poison-immune); single spawn about every 3 h. Top-tier boss for the Lv100 cap.

Someone Else's Grave (Rotten Mushmom)

Boss map · Forgotten Hollow (Classic-only) · Level 39-60 (boss Lv60) Likely

How to get there: Hidden Street off Decayed Tunnel I inside Forgotten Hollow (inner portal continues to Bygone Days). Opens with the Nov 18 update.

Why go: Rotten Mushmom (Lv60, 86,400 HP, about 850 EXP), the only place it spawns; respawn 1-1.5 h. Bring a party and potions.

Forgotten Hollow entrance (Tree Tunnel)

Secret entrance · Victoria Island - north of Ellinia · Level 39+ Likely

How to get there: Start 'Welcome to the Hollow' with Grendel the Really Old in Ellinia; go to The Forest North of Ellinia -> Forest Up North and take the Tree Tunnel down. Forgotten Entrance III has a portal back. Luma sells a Return Scroll to Forgotten Hollow for 800 mesos (Zelya's quest gives 2).

Why go: 15 quests in 4 chains, about 84k EXP and 19.5k mesos total; 10 new mobs (Lv39-59); hidden streets to Collision of Ice and Fire and The Valley of Death; Hollow PQ from Lv41 planned for Dec 9.

Utah's Pig Farm (troll map)

Hidden map / curiosity · Victoria Island - Henesys (Hidden Street) · Level Any Confirmed

How to get there: Off The Forest East of Henesys; Utah is the NPC.

Why go: Full of Lv200 Super Ribbon Pigs that give 0 EXP. Movement skills, return scrolls, Teleport Rocks and channel change are disabled. Novelty/screenshot spot, not a grind map.

The Pig Beach

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - near Lith Harbor · Level 10-20 Confirmed

How to get there: From 3-Way Road-Split (Victoria Road), take the hidden portal.

Why go: Pigs and Ribbon Pigs (leather, Pig's Ribbons for crafting); a rare Iron Hog (Lv28) on a 1 h timer needed for Weaponcrafting quest 'A Weaponcrafter in My Own Right!' (Lv25, 30 kills). Good alternative 15-20 grind.

Mushroom Garden

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - west of Henesys · Level 3-10 Confirmed

How to get there: Hidden portal from A Hill West of Henesys (leads on to Forest West of Henesys).

Why go: Very dense low-level spawns (34 spawn points, Shrooms/Red Snails/Orange Mushrooms); several beginner quests (Mai, Olaf) tick here.

Marr's Forest (Mar the Fairy)

Hidden npc area · Victoria Island - Ellinia · Level Any Likely

How to get there: Hidden Street connected to Ellinia town, next to the Magic Library/Circulation Desk.

Why go: Mar the Fairy, the pet revival NPC ('Mar the Fairy and the Water of Life' quest). Relevant because Classic pets need Water of Life every 30 days.

Thieves' Hideout (Dark Lord)

Hidden npc area · Victoria Island - Kerning City · Level Thieves 10/30; other quests 28+ Confirmed

How to get there: Single portal from Kerning City.

Why go: Dark Lord: Thief job advancements plus The Dark Lord's Signature (Lv28) and Citizenship quests (Lv57).

The Murky Swamp and Cabin of the Forsaken

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - Kerning City · Level 21-35 Confirmed

How to get there: Hidden Street from Niora Hospital (Kerning City); continues to Cabin of the Forsaken.

Why go: Jr. Neckis and Lv31 Ligators, about 110k solo EXP/h (site estimate). Breakable Witch's Herb respawns every 1.5-5 min. The Oct 15 patch fixed a rope here.

The Hermit's Cabin (Slade)

Hidden npc area · Victoria Island - Kerning swamp area · Level 57+ Likely

How to get there: At the end of Monkey Swamp I -> II -> III (Hidden Street chain).

Why go: Slade starts two Lv57 Citizenship quests ('The Weight of Truth', 'What It Takes'), each worth 17,800 EXP and 1,784 mesos. Appears to be Classic-new.

Land of Wild Boar / Over the Wall

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - Perion · Level 23-35 Confirmed

How to get there: Hidden portal from Perion Street Corner; inner portal to Over the Wall.

Why go: Wild Boars and Dark Axe Stumps, plus Jr. Boogies on 5-10 min timers; Jane's Final Challenge (100 Dark Axe Stumps). Good 21-30 alternative to KPQ.

The Breathing Rock (to Golem's Temple)

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - Henesys Dungeon · Level 19-56 Confirmed

How to get there: Henesys Dungeon Entrance -> The Breathing Rock -> The Entrance of Golem's Temple -> Golem's Temple I-IV.

Why go: Mixed Blue Mushrooms, Lupins and Lv56 Stone Golems; quest kills for Chief Stan, Jane and Ronnie. Strong party map (about 500k EXP/h with 6+, site estimate).

Caution Falling Down

Hidden hunting ground · Victoria Island - Kerning City · Level 8-20 Confirmed

How to get there: Hidden Street off Kerning City Construction Site.

Why go: Octopi and Blue Mushrooms; 7 quests progress here, including Chief Stan's and Jane's Blue Mushroom hunts.

Sleepy Dungeon V (Jr. Boogie spawns)

Deep dungeon · Victoria Island - Sleepywood · Level 33-58 Likely

How to get there: Sleepywood -> Sleepy Dungeon I -> ... -> V. It links to Forgotten Dungeon Tunnel (locked until launch).

Why go: Curse Eyes, Lupins, Dark Stone Golems, and Jr. Boogies on 5/10 min timers. Mob rate was cut to 0.4x in the Oct 15 patch.

Deep Ant Tunnel I-II / Ant Tunnel Park

Deep dungeon · Victoria Island - Sleepywood · Level 20-55 Likely

How to get there: Sleepywood -> Ant Tunnel I-IV -> Ant Tunnel Park; Deep Ant Tunnel and Dark Cave branch off, with the Mushmom grave at the end.

Why go: Ant Tunnel I is the standard Lv20-21 stop; Evil Eye and Zombie Mushroom zones lead to Zombie Mushmom.

Kerning City PQ (1st Accompaniment)

Party quest · Victoria Island - Kerning City · Level 21+ (no upper cap in Classic) Confirmed

How to get there: Party leader talks to Lakelis by the Kerning sewer entrance; exactly 4 players, channels 3-7, 30 minutes.

Why go: Stage EXP, a Companion's Magic Box per member, a chance at Squishy Shoes (Lv28) from King Slime. 'Proof of Companionship' gives a random Intermediate earring scroll and unlocks Shumi's chain. Classic changes: deaths don't end the run, multiple parties per channel.

2nd job test maps

Instanced job maps · Hidden Street · Level 30 Confirmed

How to get there: Warped in by your job instructor during the Lv30 advancement: Ant Tunnel For Bowman, Magician's Tree Dungeon, Thief's Construction Site, Warrior's Rocky Mountain.

Why go: Collect Dark Marbles for the 2nd job advancement. Thief instructor is at Construction Site North of Kerning City.

Pio's boxes and the Green Relaxer (Maple Island)

Secret / missable · Maple Island - Amherst (Rainbow Street) · Level 5 (Beginner only) Confirmed

How to get there: Pio in Amherst (Beginners, Lv5): break abandoned wooden boxes around town for 2 Rusty Screws + 2 Old Wooden Boards.

Why go: The Green Relaxer chair, 100 EXP and 150 mesos. Only obtainable on Maple Island; the boat to Lith Harbor (Lv7, 300 mesos) is one-way, so finish all island quests first.

Arwen and the Glass Shoe (repeatable)

Repeatable quest · Victoria Island - Ellinia / Perion · Level 29+ Confirmed

How to get there: Arwen the Fairy in Ellinia; farm Fire Boars near Perion for her Glass Shoe.

Why go: 4,430 EXP and 921 mesos per turn-in, plus a random Fairy Wing or Piece of Ice (crafting or quest material).

Free Market Entrance (11 rooms)

Hub · Hidden Street · Level Any Confirmed

How to get there: Free Market Entrance portals in the major towns.

Why go: Player shops (Store Permits). Classic's whole economy is separate, and this is the main price-discovery spot.
